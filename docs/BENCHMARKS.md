## Upload response time

Time for `POST /accounts/:id/imports` to answer, 10,000-row statement, 20 sequential uploads each. "Synchronous" is the same staging and `processImport` awaited inside the request (a bench-only route), i.e. the design without a queue.

| Design | p50 | p95 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Async (stage + enqueue, 202) | 55.2 ms | 118.8 ms |
| Synchronous (process in request) | 945.2 ms | 1048.7 ms |

**17.1× faster** response at p50.

- 10 new files covering already-imported history: **0 rows inserted**.

## Missing-transaction detection

100 statements × 500 rows with 1–5 random interior rows removed (303 rows, 303 gaps), plus 20 untouched statements.

- Gaps detected: **303 / 303**
- Gaps where the reported missing amount was exact to the paisa: **303 / 303**
- False alarms: **0**

Limitation: a missing *first* or *last* row can't be detected, because the statement's opening/closing balance is derived from those rows. Uploading an overlapping statement covers it.
